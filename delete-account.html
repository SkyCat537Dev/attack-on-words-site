Delete your Attack on Words account

Option 1: in the game (instant)

  1. Open Settings from the main menu.
  2. Scroll to the account section and tap Delete next to "Delete account".
  3. Tap it again to confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the game, email skyproductionsmc@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account, with the subject "Delete my Attack on Words account" and, if you remember it, your username. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address, Google name and account ID)
  • Your username, trophies, level and leaderboard entry
  • Your cloud save
  • Your friends list, friend requests and invites

Temporary matchmaking and online-count data is not tied to your account and disappears on its own within minutes.

What stays

Progress stored on your own device is not on our servers. It is removed when you uninstall the game. Deletion cannot be undone.
