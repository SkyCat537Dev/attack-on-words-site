Privacy Policy

Attack on Words · Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what the game "Attack on Words" ("the game") collects, why, and how you can have it deleted. The game is made by an individual developer, contactable at skyproductionsmc@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You can play offline against bots without signing in. In that case your progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your device and we receive nothing about you.

What we collect if you sign in or play online

  • Account information from Google Sign-In: your email address, your Google display name and a unique account ID. Your email and Google name are never shown to other players.
  • Username that you choose. It is shown to other players in online matches, on the leaderboard and in friend lists.
  • Game data: your cloud save (progress, unlocked words, stats, playtime and preferences), trophies and level.
  • Social data: friend requests, friends and game invites.
  • Online activity: a random per-launch ID used to count how many players are online, and temporary matchmaking data (room codes) while you wait for or play an online match. This is deleted automatically after a short time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising ID. The game has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your progress backed up across devices.
  • To run online features: matchmaking, the leaderboard, friends and invites.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and authentication. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Google handles the Sign in with Google step. See Google's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Temporary matchmaking and online-count data is removed automatically within minutes.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings → Delete account. This immediately removes your account, username, cloud save, trophies and friends from our servers. You can also request deletion on the web, see how to delete your account. Progress stored on your device is removed by uninstalling the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us at skyproductionsmc@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
